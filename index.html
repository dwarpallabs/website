LinkGuard

An Android app that sits between the links you tap in WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS and your browser. Safe links open straight away. If a link looks like a scam, you see one clear warning in English, Hindi or Tamil, and you can still choose to open it.

LinkGuard never logs or sends the links you open. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
