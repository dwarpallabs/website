LinkGuard

Check every link before it opens.

An Android app that sits between the links you tap in WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS and your browser. Safe links open straight away. If a link looks like a scam, you see one clear warning in English or any of India’s 22 official languages, and you can still choose to open it.

Download LinkGuard 0.3.1 Source code on GitHub

Pre-release · Android 8 and up

How it works

  1. You tap a link

    In WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS, the way you always do.

  2. LinkGuard checks it

    In a split second, against the tricks scammers use and a daily-updated list of tens of thousands of known scam websites. It never saves or sends the links you open.

  3. Safe links open straight away

    In your own browser. If a link looks like a scam, you see a warning first.
