How it works

  1. You tap a link

    In WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS, the way you always do.

  2. LinkGuard checks it

    In a split second, against the tricks scammers use and a daily-updated list of known scam websites.

  3. Safe links open straight away

    In your own browser, so you only ever see LinkGuard when a link looks like a scam.

What it warns about

LinkGuard looks for fake bank and brand websites, app downloads from outside the Play Store, UPI payment requests, brand-new websites, unusual web addresses and known scam websites. You always decide: every warning has an “Open anyway” button.

  • This link may be a scam

    For links that are very likely scams, such as a website pretending to be your bank.

  • Be careful with this link

    For links that look unusual, or that other LinkGuard users have reported as a scam this week.

  • Safe links: nothing at all

    The link just opens in your browser, with no pop-up and no delay you would notice.

Privacy in plain words

LinkGuard never saves or sends the links you open. No account needed. It uses the internet only for these checks:

  • Short links. Asks services like bit.ly where a short link really goes.
  • Brand-new websites. Asks the official registry when the website was created. Only the website name is sent, never the full link.
  • Known scam websites. Sends only a short scrambled code made from the website name. Many websites share each code, so no one can tell which website you opened.
  • Reports. Only when you tap Send report. Your report can’t be traced back to you.
